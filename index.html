Adrian Cross woke to the sound of his baby crying. He rushed into Lyra's room. Her crib was empty. The window was open, and snow covered the floor. Adrian looked outside and saw tiny footprints leading into the forest. Beside the window was a black pendant shaped like a crown. Adrian grabbed it and ran outside. He followed the footprints until he reached an abandoned mountain settlement. A church stood at the center of the village. The bell suddenly rang. Then Adrian heard Lyra crying. He ran toward the sound.
